# Literature entries

The plugin turns paper identifiers into literature entries in an Obsidian note.

## Language

**Paper identifier**:
The original input that identifies a paper, such as a DOI, arXiv ID, or official USENIX presentation URL.

**ACM catalog ID**:
An identifier used in the ACM Digital Library. A DOI-shaped catalog ID does not by itself prove that Crossref or doi.org has a registered record.

**Citation count**:
The number of citations reported by a named provider when queried. Crossref and Semantic Scholar can report different counts for the same paper.

**Literature entry**:
A paper's formatted bibliographic information in a note, followed by any notes written by the reader.

**Tracked entry**:
A literature entry with a dedicated citation link. Its link identifies both the paper and the count provider after the placeholder is replaced.

**Legacy entry**:
A literature entry without a dedicated citation link or a convertible identity comment. Its ordinary links and displayed citation count do not establish a trusted count provider.
